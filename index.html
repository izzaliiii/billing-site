Common questions

Before we talk.

Have a question about scope or getting started? Here are a few useful starting points.

Can you work with our current EHR or practice management system?

Compatibility depends on your current systems and the workflows you need. We would review those details with you before proposing an approach.

Do we have to outsource our entire billing operation?

Not necessarily. The right scope depends on your team, goals, and where you need support. We can discuss a focused or broader arrangement.

How do you handle onboarding?

We begin by mapping your current process, systems, and responsibilities. Then we agree on the transition plan, communication cadence, and reporting before work begins.

What information should I bring to an introductory call?

A general overview of your specialty, practice size, current billing setup, and the challenges you want to address is a helpful start. Please do not submit patient or other sensitive information through this page.
